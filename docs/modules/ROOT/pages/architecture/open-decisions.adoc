= Decisions of 2026-11-05
:description: The developer's decisions on the API, access control, the metamodel and the generators, taken on 2026-11-05, with the options that were weighed.

The developer reviewed the metamodel on 2026-11-05 and asked for:

* a richer metamodel;
* mandatory IRIs, labels and definitions;
* an access model of its own;
* fine-grained access in this iteration;
* generated artefacts placed in the right packages;
* a lighter API.

Three research passes produced the pages linked below. The developer took every decision on the same
day. Status of each: *Accepted* (developer, 2026-11-05).

Decided with the request itself:

* The access model lives in rdf-persistence as its own module; applications import it and keep their
  access rules in a module apart from the domain.
* Models must carry IRIs, labels and definitions, checked at generation.
* SKOS, DCTerms, PROV-O and ODRL are the default annotation vocabularies.
* The generator produces Python and TypeScript classes, diagrams, shapes and the JSON-LD context,
  each in the package that uses it; LinkML generators are extended where needed.
* JSON-LD and GraphQL are considered now, not deferred.

|D-1
|Which API contract? (xref:architecture/api.adoc[API])
|A. JSON-LD documents from a generic router. B. Plain JSON, typed by classes, with the context by `Link` header. C. GraphQL with the JSON-LD context. D. JSON:API from configuration.
|*A and B together*: one generic router driven by the schema, plain JSON by default, JSON-LD and Turtle by content negotiation. *C is the backup* for a later implementation.

|D-2

|*Our own hook*, and a short spike of linkml-openapi.

|D-4

|A. Our own check over ODRL-shaped grants. B. A with OpenFGA. C. A plus property-level rules. D. Fluree's native policies.
|*A*: our own ODRL specification in RDF and our own evaluation. *OpenFGA deferred*, unless a cheap opportunity to reuse it appears.

|D-5

|*One model.*

|D-6

|*On aggregate roots and owned classes*, with the opt-out `rp_unlabelled: true`.

|D-7
|Who writes created, modified and creator?
|The session, about the graph IRI. The application, about the entity.
|*The session, about the graph IRI*; PROV-O derivation stays the application's.

|D-8

|*Mermaid*, with small diagrams (one module each) to keep them readable.

|D-9

|*A*: LinkML is the single source of specification; every model artefact is generated by default or toolkit-extended generators; nothing that restates the model is hand-written or edited.

== What changed in architecture v1

* Decision O-1 narrows to domain services (D-2); O-2 widens to content negotiation (D-1).
* JSON-LD and Turtle by content negotiation and per-aggregate access are v1; GraphQL and OpenFGA are
  deferred, as backups.
* The metamodel splits into modules (xref:architecture/generation.adoc#revision[model to code]).
* Epics for this toolkit are planned in this repository (D-12), not in the consumer's.
* LinkML is the single source of specification (D-13): the toolkit's own data types are declared in
  LinkML too, gaps in the stock generators are closed by toolkit extensions, and generated output is
  guarded against staleness and hand edits
  (xref:architecture/generation.adoc#guarding[model to code]).
