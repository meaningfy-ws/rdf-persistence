= Architecture decision records
:description: The settled architecture decisions of rdf-persistence that outlive the change that took them, one record each.

A record here is a decision that outlives its change. Each says the context, the decision, its
status, its consequences and the alternatives weighed. Decisions are kept in three places:

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Where |What it holds |Ids
|This page |Settled decisions that outlive the change that took them, one record each |`ADR-001` and on
|xref:architecture/open-decisions.adoc[Decision log of 2026-11-05] |The developer's decisions taken before the records began, with the options weighed; the xref:architecture/index.adoc#decisions-taken[architecture overview] adds O-1 and O-2 |`D-1` to `D-13`
|Each change's `design.md` in `openspec/changes/<id>/`, moved to `openspec/changes/archive/` when the change is done |The decisions of one change, cited by its commits (`Refs: <change-id> <task> PD-3`) |`PD-1` and on, per change
|===

A record names the earlier decision it supersedes, narrows or widens.

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Record |Decision |Status

|The Fuseki adapter is our own async httpx client, not rdflib's `SPARQLStore` or SPARQLWrapper. Supersedes the client clause of research DEC-4.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17)
