= Architecture decision records
:description: The settled architecture decisions of rdf-persistence that outlive the change that took them, one record each.

A record here is a decision that outlives its change. Each says the context, the decision, its
status, its consequences and the alternatives weighed. The decisions of work in progress are in each
change's `design.md` (`PD-` ids, archived under `openspec/changes/archive/`), and the earlier ones
are in xref:architecture/open-decisions.adoc[Open decisions].

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Record |Decision |Status

|xref:adr/adr-001-sparql-client.adoc[ADR-001]
|The Fuseki adapter is our own async httpx client, not rdflib's `SPARQLStore` or SPARQLWrapper. Supersedes the client clause of research DEC-4.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17)

|xref:adr/adr-002-jwks-cache.adoc[ADR-002]
|The token verifier keeps its own JWKS cache over PyJWT, not `PyJWKClient`.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17)

|xref:adr/adr-003-fastapi-not-connexion.adoc[ADR-003]
|FastAPI with an OpenAPI document generated from LinkML, not connexion.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17)

|xref:adr/adr-004-signing-algorithms.adoc[ADR-004]
|The verifier's algorithms and clock leeway are parameters; classical algorithms by default; post-quantum follows the identity provider.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17)

|xref:adr/adr-005-representations.adoc[ADR-005]
|One aggregate is served in seven representations; a listing stays JSON. Narrows research DEC-27, widens D-1.
|Accepted (developer, 2026-11-17); the routes are the leader's choice
|===
