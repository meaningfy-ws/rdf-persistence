# EPIC: Manual revision notes

> Candidate EPIC, opened 2026-11-16 to collect the improvement opportunities the developer finds
> while revising rdf-persistence by hand. Not shaped yet: the notes in inputs/seed.md are the
> source; appetite, scope and the PLAN (design.md, tasks.md) follow once they are triaged.

## Why

A manual revision of the toolkit turns up improvement opportunities that belong to no open EPIC.
This change records them in one place, so each can be triaged: folded into an existing EPIC,
shaped here, or dropped.

## What changes

To be shaped from the inputs:

- `inputs/seed.md`: the developer's notes, verbatim.
- `inputs/analysis.md`: findings per note, with the open questions Q-1 to Q-6.
- `inputs/improvements.md`: the candidate changes, grouped:
  - A: defects
  - B: reuse a library
  - C: constants
  - D: models instead of dicts
  - E: thematic structure
  - F: generators as their own distribution
  - G: decisions to record
  - H: separate EPICs
- `inputs/keep.md`: what stays, and why.

The developer's answers to Q-1 to Q-6 come first. Groups E and F are large and are likely to
become EPICs of their own.

## Appetite

Open until the notes are triaged.
